🏦 How to Use Wolverine Grader Pro

  1. 1
    Choose your AI provider & model

    Pick Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini. Select a model from the dropdown — each provider lists links to grab a free or paid API key. Paste your key in and check “Remember” so you only have to do this once.

  2. 2
    Set your grading strictness

    Drag the slider from Effort Counts! (generous) to College Prof Who Hates You (maximum rigor). This controls how harshly the AI applies the rubric.

  3. 3
    Provide a rubric

    Paste it as text, upload a .docx/.pdf/.txt file, or use ✨ Build with AI to describe what you want and let the AI generate one for you.

  4. 4
    Load the student essays

    Three options: upload a ZIP file (e.g. downloaded from Canvas), select individual files from your computer via drag-and-drop or file picker, or connect to Canvas LMS directly to pull submissions from any assignment in any course.

  5. 5
    Start Grading

    Hit the button and watch the AI work through each essay in real time. A progress log shows each student as they’re graded.

  6. 6
    Review scores & feedback

    Navigate through students one by one. The AI-suggested score is editable — just click and type. The feedback comment is also fully editable before anything goes to students.

  7. 7
    Choose your feedback detail level

    Toggle between Summary Only, Scores + Summary (shows points per rubric category), or Full Breakdown (per-category scores with explanations). The AI already processed everything — no re-grading needed.

  8. 8
    Send grades to students

    Use Copy for Canvas to copy a student’s score and feedback to your clipboard for pasting into SpeedGrader — or if you connected via Canvas LMS, hit 🎓 Push to Canvas to send the grade and comment directly. No copy-paste required.

  9. 🎉
    Enjoy your extra free time.

    You just graded a full class batch in minutes instead of hours. Go outside.
